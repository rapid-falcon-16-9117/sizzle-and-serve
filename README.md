**Sizzle & Serve** is a fast little diner game played in a single 90-second shift. Order tickets slide onto the rail (up to three at a time), each asking for one of four burgers — a Classic Beef Burger, a Double Cheese Burger, a Garden Fresh Burger, or a Bacon & Egg Breakfast Burger — built from eight ingredients: bun, beef patty, cheese, lettuce, tomato, fried egg, bacon and onion. The catch is the order of the stack: the plate must match the ticket exactly, bottom to top. Beef patties can't go on raw — put them on one of the two grill slots and watch the progress ring: pull them when they're cooked, because a burnt patty goes in the bin. Serve a correct burger (click Serve or press Space) to earn its price in coins plus speed and streak bonuses; let a ticket's timer run out and you lose points. When the shift ends you get a score, a served count and a chef rating. Play with the mouse, or keys 1–8 to grab ingredients, Space to serve and P to pause. Pure vanilla JavaScript and Canvas 2D — no assets, no network, everything is drawn in code.

## Run it

```
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a browser. (Node 22 or newer; no dependencies to install.)
